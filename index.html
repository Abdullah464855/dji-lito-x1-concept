Built for two different buyers.

Same drone family, two right answers — it depends what you will do with the footage.

For pure beginners

Simple controls, flagship safety, and a price that forgives mistakes. If you will post straight from the drone and never grade footage, the Lito 1 at £299 is enough — same safety, same flight time.

Lito 1 — from £299 →

For creators who edit

A bigger sensor for dusk flights, D-Log M colour with real grading room, LiDAR up front, and 42GB built in so the memory card stops being a failure point. The X1 at £369.

CAMERA

A bigger sensor, a brighter lens, colour you can actually grade.

The 1/1.3-inch sensor with an f/1.7 lens (24mm equivalent) gathers more light than the Lito 1's 1/2-inch f/1.8, so dusk shots stay cleaner. 4K/60fps HDR with up to 14 stops holds bright sky and dark ground in one frame. Not the Mini 5 Pro's 1-inch sensor — the best camera in the beginner class, and that is the claim.

3-axis gimbal 48MP stills 4K/100fps slow-mo 2.7K vertical
SENSING

It sees the branch the camera cannot.

Both Litos brake or bypass obstacles in every direction with omnidirectional vision down to 5 lux. The X1 adds forward-facing LiDAR for small, low-contrast obstacles ahead — trees, wires, dusk. Not crash-proof; the forward direction is simply sharper.

Omnidirectional vision Forward LiDAR Works down to 5 lux ActiveTrack 12 m/s
STORAGE

42GB built in. The card stops being a failure point.

The Lito 1 records nothing without a microSD card. The X1 records straight to 42GB internal storage, still takes a card for more, and pulls files off-state with QuickTransfer at up to 50MB/s — no card, no reader, no formatting moment before a shoot.

42GB internal microSD slot QuickTransfer 50MB/s

Flight times are lab ratings; real-world flying is shorter. The Plus battery takes takeoff weight over 249g — see FAQ.

Questions, answered straight.

If you will post straight from the drone and never grade footage, the Lito 1 at £299 is enough — same safety, same flight time. If you edit video, shoot at dusk, or hate managing memory cards, the X1's £70 buys a bigger sensor, LiDAR, D-Log M and 42GB built in. That is the whole decision.

A 1-inch sensor, a telephoto lens, and the flagship flight time of the Mavic class — those arrive in Week 2 of this series, starting with the Mavic 4 Pro on Day 8.

Under 249g it sits in the sub-250g class in many countries — but rules differ by country, and the Plus battery takes it over 249g. Check your local rules before you fly; in Nigeria, check NCAA requirements.

Not officially — the Lito series is not sold through DJI's official US store, so US buyers face grey-market channels. Check a retailer that ships to you before promising a price.

Rated up to 36 minutes in lab conditions (52 with the Plus battery). Real-world mixed flying is less — plan shoots around roughly two-thirds of the rating.

£70. One decision. No regrets either way.
